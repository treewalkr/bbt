Using bbt by touch

Sort the list

  • Tap a torrent to pick it. Tap it again for details and screenshots.
  • The four buttons at the bottom sort the picked torrent and move to the next one.
  • Mark N viewed on a day sorts the whole day. Above 20 it asks you to tap Confirm.
  • Made a mistake? Tap Undo in the message at the bottom. Each tap goes one change further back.

Swipe mode

  • Open it from View, then Swipe.
  • Swipe the card left to pass (Viewed), up for later (View later), right to keep (Download later).
  • Tap the left or right side of the picture to flip screenshots. The corner button shows one full screen.

Screenshots and privacy

  • Pinch to zoom a screenshot, drag to move it.
  • Posters and screenshots start blurred. Tap Show to see one; View turns blur on or off.

Keyboard

Click a key to change it.

Settings

Sync

Only if the last sync is over an hour old. Each sync reads at most 12 pages, 3 seconds apart, and waits 10 minutes before the next one. Last sync: Never

List

Swipe mode

Screenshots for the next cards load while you look at this one. Each card asks the tracker for one page, one at a time, 3 seconds apart, and never again once saved. None asks only for a card you stay on.

Keyboard

To change a key, click ? at the top right, then click the key. Turn off if speech input or a screen reader sends stray keys; arrows, Enter and Esc keep working.
